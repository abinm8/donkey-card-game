Common questions

How do I play?

Tap the ? on the welcome screen, or How to play on the level screen, for a quick illustrated guide. The short version: follow the suit of the first card; if you can't, play any card to strike, and the highest card of the suit picks up the pile. The last player holding cards is the Donkey.

How do I save my progress?

Sign in with Apple or Google, either on the welcome screen or in Settings → Account. Your coins and unlocked tables are then saved to your account, and come back when you sign in on a new phone or after reinstalling.

I reinstalled and lost my coins

If you played as a guest, your progress lived only on the phone and was removed with the app. If you'd signed in, sign in again with the same Apple or Google account and it will come back.

How do I delete my account?

In the game, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and its saved progress from our servers. See the Privacy policy for details.

How do I turn off music, sound or vibration?

Open Settings (the gear on the level screen) and switch them off. The iPhone's silent switch also mutes the game.

Can I buy coins?

No. Coins are for play only. You earn them by winning games and from the daily gift. They can't be bought, sold or exchanged for money.
