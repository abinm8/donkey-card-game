Privacy Policy

Donkey Card Game ("the game", "we") is a card game for iPhone. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can delete it. In short: you can play without giving us anything, and if you choose to sign in, we keep only what's needed to save your progress.

Playing as a guest

If you choose Play as Guest, nothing leaves your phone. Your donkey name, avatar, coins, unlocked tables and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Signing in (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or Google to save your progress and restore it on another phone. When you do, we receive and store:

  • An account identifier from Apple or Google, so we know it's you next time.
  • Your email address, as shared by Apple or Google. With Sign in with Apple you can choose Hide My Email, and we then receive only a private relay address.
  • Your first name, only to suggest a donkey name the first time. We don't keep it unless you choose to use it as your donkey name.
  • Your game progress: your donkey name and avatar, coins, unlocked tables and which table intros you've seen.

We use this only to save and restore your progress. We don't use it for advertising, we don't build a profile of you, and we never sell or share it.

Where your data is stored

Signed-in accounts and saved progress are stored with Supabase, our database provider, on servers in the United States. Each player's progress can be read and changed only by that player's own signed-in account. Sign-in itself is handled by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising, and no advertising or tracking identifiers.
  • No analytics or behaviour tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • No payment information. Coins in the game are for play only: they can't be bought, sold or exchanged for money or anything of value.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and the progress saved with it from our servers. Your phone keeps its own copy as a guest until you delete the app.

You can also ask us to delete your data, or ask what we hold about you, using the contact details on our Support page.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If the game starts doing something new with your data, for example showing ads, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that change goes live.
