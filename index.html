HOW TO PLAY

Don't be the Donkey.

A classic Kerala card game, played in homes, clubs and chaayakadas for generations. Five players, one deck, and a single loser left holding the cards.

01

Follow the suit

The Ace of Spades starts. Everyone plays the suit of the first card. If all follow, the cards are thrown away.

02

Strike!

No card of that suit? Play any other. The round stops, and the highest card of the suit picks up the whole pile.

03

Empty your hand

Get rid of every card to go safe. The last player still holding cards is the Donkey.

THE REGULARS

Twenty characters. Zero mercy.

From cheeky kids on summer holidays to the card sharks of the toddy shop, everyone has a face for every hand: smug when they strike, sulking when they pick up the pile.

Hardcore mode

At the Kallu Shaap nothing is enforced. Lie about your suit, play out of turn, and call a cheat if you catch one.

Smart opponents

Bots only see what a real player would. The higher the table, the more carefully they count the cards.
